- **A decode/schema failure now falls back to the next provider before a retry is counted.** An unreadable boolean/number reply or JSON that fails its `schema` is treated as a failed attempt in the `provider_fallbacks` chain: it's recorded in `meta.fallback_attempts` (with a size-capped `raw_reply` and a `decode_failed`/`schema_invalid` status) and the next provider is tried before the prompt's `retries` budget is spent — a small local model that keeps answering "maybe" to a yes/no prompt now reaches a configured fallback instead of exhausting its retries on itself. With no `provider_fallbacks` configured, behaviour is unchanged.
