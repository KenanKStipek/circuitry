- **idea-farm submission backpressure.** The `cyberdiner` adapter gained an
  opt-in `max_in_flight` cap (`runtime.adapters.cyberdiner.max_in_flight`): a
  caller past the cap blocks until a job completes instead of bursting every
  submission at the fleet at once. The idea-farm service wires it via a new
  `MAX_IN_FLIGHT_JOBS` env var (default `4`, below `theme_rounds`'
  `max_concurrency: 8`) so rounds no longer outrun CyberDiner's claim window
  and die `TimedOut`. Its run-summary log line now also reports
  `gap_ideas=ran` or `gap_ideas=skipped (no content)`, distinguishing a
  healthy skip of the rescue round from an actual failure.
