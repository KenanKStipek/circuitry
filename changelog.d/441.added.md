- **electricity:** `electricity <config.json> <doc>` now resolves the config file, seeds and
  checks the document, refuses unsupported content (including a configured persistence backend
  or runtime plugin) with the preview marker, and runs it — writing `--out` on success and failure
  alike, `--events` (format v1) and `--live-state` (coalesced, atomic, equal to `--out` at the
  end), and following `cof`'s own non-TTY stdout contract (Circuitry's own `ok`/`error`/`warnings`/
  `state_out` shape matches exactly, and its own `Warning:`/`WARNING:` stderr lines are
  reproduced for every site reachable from M0-H's own supported subset, with a few still-open
  gaps documented in `electricity/DESIGN.md` §6.9 -- #442's "Warning lines on stderr" item).
  SIGINT/SIGTERM/SIGHUP
  cancel a run (exit 130/143/129), armed on a dedicated thread from before config load, with a
  second SIGINT/SIGTERM during cleanup exiting immediately and SIGHUP honouring `SIG_IGN`
  (`nohup`) and never counting as that second signal.
