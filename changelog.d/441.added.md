- **electricity:** `electricity <config.json> <doc>` now resolves the config file, seeds and
  checks the document, refuses unsupported content with the preview marker, and (once lanes B/C
  land a real interpreter) runs it — writing `--out` on success and failure alike, `--events`
  (format v1) and `--live-state` (coalesced, atomic, equal to `--out` at the end), and following
  `cof`'s own non-TTY stdout contract. SIGINT/SIGTERM/SIGHUP cancel a run (exit 130/143/129), with
  a second SIGINT/SIGTERM during cleanup exiting immediately and SIGHUP honouring `SIG_IGN`
  (`nohup`) and never counting as that second signal.
