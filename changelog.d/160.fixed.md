- **`cof run --decompose-out` now persists rejected planner payloads.** A
  `planner_failed` decomposition whose planner actually returned a payload
  that failed the `{say, chunks, yaml}` envelope schema (e.g. a bare JSON
  array) is written to disk as `<run_id>__<effect_path>.rejected.yml` (or
  `.rejected.txt` when the payload doesn't parse as YAML/JSON) — that raw
  payload was previously discarded even though it was the most readable
  record of what went wrong. A planner failure with no payload at all
  (timeout, adapter error) still writes nothing.
