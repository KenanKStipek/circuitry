- **`prime.<loop>.last` — the last-iteration spelling in the loop grammar.**
  After a named loop completes, `prime.<loop>.last.<step>.value` (and deeper
  field paths) resolve against the final *completed* iteration's node — same
  shape as `iter_<N>`, without having to guess an `N` that is unknowable for
  `while` loops and data-dependent `each` loops. Passes that errored under
  `on_error: continue`/`break` are skipped in favor of the last one that
  finished, and a zero-iteration loop writes no `last` key, so reads fall
  through exactly like a missing `iter_<N>`. In-body or in-condition use
  lint-warns on the validation warnings channel (it resolves to the previous
  pass at best — `prime.<step>.value` is the current pass). The
  `patterns/critique_refine_loop` pattern's `final` step now reads
  `prime.review.last.refine_step.value.refined` — the last refinement, where
  it previously pinned `iter_0` (the first) as a workaround.
