- **Aliases of any adapter.** A `runtime.adapters.<alias>` entry with `adapter: <name>` now works for every built-in adapter except `host_claude` (before: `claude_code` and `pi` only), e.g. several OpenAI-compatible endpoints in one config, each with its own `base_url`, `default_model` and `timeout_seconds`. An alias uses its target's credential.
