- **`runtime.complexity` config block.** A validated, documented config surface
  for complexity-aware execution with three independent switches — `scoring`,
  `routing` and `decomposition` — all off by default. Malformed band tables,
  non-numeric thresholds, and unknown keys fail at config resolution with a
  message naming the offending path, and enabling `routing` or `decomposition`
  without `scoring` reports the missing prerequisite. The resolved block is
  exposed as typed settings on `EffectiveSettings.complexity` (via
  `circuitry.cli.complexity_config.resolve_complexity_settings`) and its winning
  layer is recorded in the effective-settings source table. See
  [Complexity Configuration](docs/complexity-config.md).
