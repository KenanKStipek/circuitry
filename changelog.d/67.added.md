- `cof run` and `cof run-library` accept `--adapter <name>` and `--model <name>`, overriding the adapter/model for a single invocation. Flags sit at the top of the precedence chain (above `--profile`, the orchestration, `CIRCUITRY_ADAPTER`/`CIRCUITRY_MODEL`, and config files) and are recorded in `runtime.effective_settings.sources` as `cli`. `cof run --last` replays them.
