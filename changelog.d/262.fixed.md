- **A templated `false` boolean param no longer coerces to `true`.** Mustache
  renders a templated `false` as the literal string `"False"`, and Python's
  `bool("False")` is `True` — every plugin site that coerced a boolean param
  with bare `bool(params.get(...))` silently inverted a templated-false
  value. `env_vars`'s `include_secrets` was the most sensitive instance:
  `include_secrets: "{{input.reveal}}"` with `reveal=false` revealed secrets
  anyway. A shared, string-aware `_as_bool()` (`plugins/base.py`) now backs
  every one of these sites — `env_vars`, `email_smtp`, `shell`/`_subprocess`'s
  `allow_nonzero`, `csv`, `gzip`, `xml`, `gpg`, `diff_patch` and others —
  treating `"false"`/`"False"`/`"0"`/`"no"`/`""` as `False`.
