- **`cyberdiner` adapter — expo's server-side claim timeout now ends the poll loop.** When no cook claims a job, expo's timeout service retires it after ~5 minutes (`job.timed_out`, `reason=claim_timeout`). That status was not in the adapter's terminal set, so it kept polling a job expo had already declared dead until its own `timeout_seconds` (default 600) expired — roughly doubling the cost of every claim timeout and reporting it as `timed out after 600s waiting for job N`, which blames the client's clock for a server-side outcome. The timed-out status is now terminal and raises a distinct, actionable error: `cyberdiner: job <id> timed out server-side before being claimed (reason=claim_timeout) — no cook served tier '<tier>' in time`, with expo's `errorCode`/`errorMessage` appended when present. Status matching is folded to lowercase alphanumerics, so the camelCase wire value (`timedOut`), the Rust enum spelling (`TimedOut`) and the event-name spelling (`timed_out`) are all recognized — the same defensiveness already applied to `complete`/`completed`.
