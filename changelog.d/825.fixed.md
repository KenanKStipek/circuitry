- **`osi` enters the alternate screen before it detects its engine.** `osi <doc>` used to switch to the alternate screen only after `cof run --help`/`electricity --help` capability detection (up to 10s) and the engine spawn had both already run; under heavy concurrent load, a cold `cof` start can itself take several seconds, and the two together could push osi's first visible frame past 20s. The TUI's terminal is now entered right after argument parsing, before that probe ever runs, so how soon osi shows something no longer depends on how long engine detection takes. The engine/label capability notices and a plan-compile failure, previously printed to stderr before the TUI took over, now surface as log lines inside the TUI instead, since the terminal may already be on the alternate screen by the time they're known.
