- **`electricity-template`: a chevron port for the electricity preview
  runner's Mustache templates.** A new crate
  (`electricity/crates/electricity-template`) ports chevron's tokenizer
  and renderer over `electricity-value`'s `Value` (DESIGN.md §3.3), not a
  generic Mustache engine: `{{name}}` escapes `& < > "` (never `'`),
  `{{{name}}}`/`{{&name}}` don't escape anything, dotted names and list
  indices walk nested dicts/lists and render empty on any missing
  segment, sections iterate a list (binding each element) or render once
  for a truthy non-list, inverted sections flip on falsy/empty/absent,
  comments and `{{=left right=}}` custom delimiters are supported, and
  standalone-line whitespace is trimmed exactly as chevron trims it.
  Mustache partials (`{{> name}}`) are rejected with Circuitry's own
  error, word for word, matching the Python reference's rejection since
  #355. Two confirmed chevron quirks are reproduced on purpose rather
  than smoothed over: a falsy element bound by a list section suppresses
  that whole iteration's output, literal text included, not just its
  tags; and `{{.}}` (escaped only, never `{{{.}}}`/`{{&.}}`) resolving to
  the literal `True` discards that resolution for the next scope up,
  raising `"list index out of range"` if there isn't one. `PlainCtx` and
  `JsonAwareCtx` reproduce the `params_json` splice asymmetry (Quirk
  Q2): a list/dict renders via `py_str` by default, or through a
  caller-supplied JSON serializer — taken as a parameter rather than a
  dependency on `electricity-json` (built in parallel) — for the one
  context a tool's `params_json` field renders against, which also never
  collapses an empty list/dict to `""` the way the default context does.
  A generator (`electricity/scripts/generate_template_corpus.py`) renders
  a synthetic corpus of templates and contexts through Circuitry's real
  `render_template`/`template_syntax_error`, including their error
  wrapping; the crate's tests replay it. CI regenerates the corpus and
  fails if it drifts.
