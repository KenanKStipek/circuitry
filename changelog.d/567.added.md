- **`--resume` continues inside a `use` child.** With `runtime.state.record_children: true`,
  `cof run --resume` no longer reruns an unfinished `use` child from its first effect: the
  child's finished effects are reused and it continues at its first unfinished one, with the
  same rules for chains and loops as the top level, at any depth of nested `use`. It does so
  only when the child's document (`meta.orchestration_sha256`) and resolved inputs
  (`meta.inputs`) match the saved record, and its own `expect:` did not judge the output false;
  otherwise the child runs from its start, as before.
  A child interrupted by Ctrl-C, SIGTERM or SIGHUP now keeps its record too. Without
  `record_children`, nothing changes.
