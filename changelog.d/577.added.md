- **`runtime.state.prompt_sent` keeps prompt text out of saved state.** A `prompt` effect records the text it sent in `meta.prompt_sent`, whole, however large; a document that sends many big prompts makes `--out`, `--live-state`, `--resume` and persistence carry every one. `runtime.state.prompt_sent` (config, or the document's own `runtime:` block) takes `full` (the default, as before), `sha256` (`meta.prompt_sent` is `null`) or `head:<N>` (the first N characters, then `\n[… <M> more characters not recorded]`). In every mode two new fields describe the whole text: `meta.prompt_sent_sha256` (hex SHA-256 of its UTF-8 bytes) and `meta.prompt_chars` (its length in characters). Only the record changes: the adapter, retries, fallback, complexity scoring and token estimates still see the whole text. `cof check` rejects any other value, and `head:0`, naming the key. `electricity` records the same fields.
