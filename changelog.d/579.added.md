- The `claude_code` and `pi` adapters have aliases: a `runtime.adapters.<alias>` entry with `adapter: claude_code|pi` runs that CLI with its own settings and an `env` map set in the CLI child's environment (also accepted on `runtime.adapters.claude_code` and `pi`). `provider: <alias>` and `<alias>:<model>` select it, `meta.adapter` records the alias name, `env` values never reach saved state, and the trust boundary is unchanged.
