- **`labels` on `if` and `loop` effects are no longer silently dropped.**
  The schema has accepted a `labels` object on `ConditionalEffect` and
  `LoopEffect` for a while, and `cof check` passed it, but only `dynamic`
  (#305) actually recorded it. `if` and `loop` now record `labels` on their
  own `meta.labels` the same way, for a named effect of either type.
