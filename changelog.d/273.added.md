- **`retries` and `expect:` on `tool` and `use` effects.** `tool`/`use` now
  accept `retries: {max_attempts, backoff_ms}` — same shape, default and
  exponential-backoff-with-jitter curve as a prompt's. An HTTP-family tool
  (`http`, `web_fetch`, `webhook`, `linear`) classifies a failure by status
  the way an adapter dispatch does (429/408/5xx retryable, any other 4xx
  not); every other (process) tool retries on any failure. A `use` retry
  re-runs the whole child orchestration from scratch. `expect:` adds an
  output check run after a successful attempt — either a CEL expression (a
  bare string is shorthand for `{mode: cel, expr: <string>}`) over the
  effect's own `value`/`meta` plus `state`, or `{mode: model, template:
  ...}`, which asks yes/no like a model-mode `if` on the run's own
  adapter/model and records its tokens. A false or unreadable expectation
  fails the attempt with `expect failed: <expr or template summary>`, after
  which `retries`/`on_error` apply; the outcome lands at `meta.expect`.
