- **`electricity-yaml`: a PyYAML-compatible YAML 1.1 loader for the electricity
  preview runner.** A new crate (`electricity/crates/electricity-yaml`) loads
  YAML the way Circuitry does — `yaml.safe_load` plus Circuitry's own
  duplicate-key check (`core/yaml_load.py`'s `_UniqueKeyLoader`) — into
  `Value`s, with its own composer and resolver on top of `saphyr-parser`:
  YAML 1.1 implicit scalar resolution (booleans in three casings with `y`/`n`
  excluded; hex/binary/legacy-octal/sexagesimal/underscored integers,
  arbitrary precision; floats requiring a literal `.` and a signed exponent,
  `.inf`/`.nan`, sexagesimal; nulls; naive and offset-aware timestamps),
  explicit core-schema tags (`!!str`/`!!int`/`!!float`/`!!bool`/`!!null`/
  `!!timestamp`/`!!binary`/`!!map`/`!!seq`, and the bare non-specific `!`),
  anchors and aliases, `<<:` merge keys (a single mapping, a list of mappings
  with earlier entries winning a conflict, and multiple bare `<<:` entries
  with the later one winning), the `=` tag as a load error, and single-document
  loading. Circuitry's duplicate-key error is reproduced word for word,
  citing both locations, with `<<:` exempt. `!!set`/`!!omap`/`!!pairs` and any
  unrecognized tag are refused outright (a documented, intentional
  divergence: `Value` has no `Set` or tuple variant to hold what they'd
  construct) rather than silently approximated. A golden corpus generated
  from Circuitry's real loader (`electricity/scripts/generate_yaml_corpus.py`)
  is checked in and replayed by the crate's tests; CI regenerates it and
  fails if it drifts.
