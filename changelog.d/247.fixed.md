- **Yes/no and numeric model answers are read leniently instead of misread.**
  `prompt_type: boolean`/`number` and model-mode `if`/`while` conditions
  matched replies exactly (`"yes"`, `"true"`, `"1"`, `"y"`; bare `int()`/
  `float()`), so an ordinary reply like `"Yes."` or `"42."` silently decoded
  to `null` (`boolean`/`number` prompts) or was read as **no** (`if`/`while`,
  taking the wrong branch or stopping the loop) — nothing raised, so retries
  never helped. A shared parser (`core.answers`) now strips surrounding
  quotes/markdown and trailing punctuation before reading the leading token:
  `"Yes."`, `"yes, because …"`, `"**TRUE**"`, `"Y"` parse as true; `"42."`,
  `"3.5"`, `"-1"`, `"1e3"` parse as numbers; a reply that still doesn't
  parse — `"maybe"`, `"about 42"`, `"42 degrees"` — **raises** instead of
  guessing, so `on_error` and `retries` apply. The raw
  reply is recorded on `meta.answer` for boolean/number prompts and for
  model-mode `if`/`while` (with `meta.adapter`/`meta.model` alongside it).
