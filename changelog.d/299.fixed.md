- **`--live-state` now shows a chain loop's passes while it runs.** A chain
  (sequential) loop used to publish nothing of its own node until the whole
  loop finished, then every pass at once — a long chain loop (a retry loop,
  an `each` over shots) looked stalled to anyone watching the mirror. It now
  publishes after every completed pass, the same path `flow: tree` has used
  since #291.
