- **osp's plan join.** An unnamed `if`/loop registered its own
  parent-duplicate path as an earlier chain sibling, and its branch's own
  children started from an empty earlier-siblings list instead of the
  enclosing chain's — both threw off the "every earlier sibling is
  complete" status heuristic for anything near one. An unnamed `if` with
  an `else` threaded the `then` branch's own new paths into the `else`
  branch's walk (and vice versa), so an absent sibling on the untaken side
  could never resolve to anything but a "likely running" guess, blocking
  both that branch and every chain sibling after the `if` from ever
  reaching one themselves; an absent sibling on the untaken side of an
  `if`/`else` now resolves once any path on the taken side is observed. A
  leaf with no distinguishing `meta` field (`use`, `yield`) showed the
  generic kind `effect` in both `--log` and the status table, even with a
  compiled plan available, instead of its own kind from the plan (part of
  #433).
