- **`runtime.state.parsed_stdout: drop` keeps a parsed step's raw stdout out
  of saved state.** With `parse: json`, a `shell`, `git`, `gh` (or other
  subprocess-family) step records its stdout twice: parsed on `value` and raw
  on `meta.stdout`. Under `drop`, a step whose stdout parsed records
  `meta.stdout: null` plus `meta.stdout_chars` (its length in characters) and
  `meta.stdout_sha256`; `value` is unchanged. `full` is the default and
  today's behaviour. A failed parse already records no stdout, so `drop`
  leaves failures unchanged, and a step without `parse: json` keeps its
  stdout. Like `prompt_sent`, the key sits under `runtime.state` (config, or
  the document's own `runtime:` block); `cof check` rejects any other value,
  naming the key. `--resume` works on a state written with `drop`.
  electricity records the same fields. See [Recording parsed
  stdout](docs/orchestration-reference.md#recording-parsed-stdout-runtimestateparsed_stdout).
