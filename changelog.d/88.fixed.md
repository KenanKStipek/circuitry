- **Loop bodies have a portable within-iteration sibling reference.** A chained
  body — step 2 reads step 1 — previously had no spelling that worked in every
  loop: `{{prime.<step>.value}}` resolved only in unnamed loops, the bare
  `{{<step>.value}}` only in named ones, and adding or removing a loop's
  `name:` silently broke every body template the other way. Both forms now
  resolve everywhere, through a scope chain (current iteration → enclosing
  scope → root state) applied in named and unnamed loops, `chain` and `tree`
  flow, and — new — in `while` conditions, which see the pass that just
  finished under the same names. `cof validate` gained two warnings for the
  spellings that misbehave silently: `iter_<N>` used inside the loop it names
  (renders pass N's stale output in every pass, rather than failing) and
  `prime.<loop>.<step>.value` (never resolves; `prime.<loop>` holds `iter_<N>`,
  `collected` and `meta`). The bundled `patterns/critique_refine_loop` had the
  first of those bugs and now reads its critique from the current pass.
