- **Run-wide concurrency limits.** `runtime.max_concurrency` caps how many `tool`/`prompt` effects dispatch at once across a *whole* run — every tree loop, parallel dynamic and `use` child now shares one pool, not just its own container's fan-out. `runtime.concurrency_groups: {name: N}` adds named resource-group caps on top of it; a leaf effect joins one with its own `group: <name>` field. Only `tool`/`prompt` ever hold a slot — `cof check` rejects `group:` on a container (`dynamic`, `loop`, `use`, `if`, `reflector`) and rejects a `group:` naming something `runtime.concurrency_groups` doesn't define. A blocked effect shows `meta.waiting_for` (`"global"` or the group name) in `--live-state` and the verbose CLI output while it waits (#274).
