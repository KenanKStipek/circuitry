- **cyberdiner adapter: real tier names.** The adapter rejected every tier a real CyberDiner network serves — it validated `model:` against an invented `tier-1`…`tier-4` list. Tier vocabulary now belongs to the network: any non-empty `model:` is submitted as-is and expo's validation is the authority (its `400` surfaces through the adapter's actionable HTTP error path). `default_tier` now defaults to `cheap`, and the bundled `learn/cyberdiner_hello` example, README, and demo runbook use real tiers (`cheap`, `fast-cheap`, `fast`, `good-cheap`, `good`, `good-fast`, `alpha`). New optional `runtime.adapters.cyberdiner.valid_tiers` opts into client-side validation against your own list.
