- **TUI profile editor no longer drops per-effect `routing` overrides on save.** `EffectOverride` was hard-coded to `model`/`provider`/`enabled`, so opening a profile that carried `effects.<path>.routing` (added in #148) and saving it silently dropped the key. `routing: false` and `routing: "<band>"` now round-trip through load, save, and `duplicate` unchanged.
