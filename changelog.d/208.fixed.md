- **`python_eval`: subscript and augmented assignment no longer raise `NameError`.** RestrictedPython rewrites `x[k] = v` to `_write_(x)[k] = v` and `n += 1` to `n = _inplacevar_('+=', n, 1)`; the plugin now wires up `_write_` (`full_write_guard`) and `_inplacevar_` guards, so item assignment on `list`/`dict` and augmented assignment of names work as expected, while writes to non-container objects and in-place mutation of protected objects via `+=`/`-=`/etc. are still rejected. `inputs` are also now mirrored into the sandbox globals so they resolve correctly inside nested comprehensions, not just at the top level.
