- **A retried `use` keeps every attempt.** When a `use` ran more than once, `meta.attempts` lists each attempt, oldest first: its `value`, `error`, `expect` result, `child_errors` and start and end times, plus its child's effects (`children`) when `runtime.state.record_children` is on. The proposals an `expect` turned down are now in the run's state, and while the `use` retries, its own `expect` and `inputs` can read the attempts before the current one (#659).
