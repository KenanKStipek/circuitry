- osi's Flow screen (`2`) is now the rail, replacing the tree: stations run left to right in plan order, forking into lanes at a decision, a loop or a parallel `dynamic`, and rejoining. A decision forks into the taken branch and the untaken one's own ghost lane (`· <else-name>`/`not taken`) — a named `if` still reads its branch from `meta.branch`; an unnamed one, which writes no state node of its own, has its decision spliced in and inferred the same way the tree it replaces already did (including inside a named loop's own pass, and two unnamed `if`s sharing one host getting their own distinct paths), is selectable the same way a named one is, and joins the Story pane with the same branch and the reason it was inferred. A loop forks into one lane per pass, never per concurrency slot, the observed passes first and then an ahead lane per further pass the loop's own count promises (read from the plan itself before any pass has even started); a parallel `dynamic` forks into one lane per named child the same way, while a sequential one (`use`, a chain-flow `dynamic`, a `try` body with its own `finally`) inlines its own children as plain stations instead. Below 100 columns, or a loop four or more lanes wide, those lanes fold into one double-rail band; `+`/`-` open or fold the *selected* fork's own lanes three at a time, never every loop at once, and an open fork never shows more than three lanes at once either way. Selecting a pass, a station or a decision drives a Selected pane the same shape the Tape's own full-screen step view already has; a Story pane lists the run's own notable events, including a synthesized line for each loop starting. `←`/`→` scroll the rail and turn follow off; `↑`/`↓`/`j`/`k` move the selected pass, turning follow off the same way and back on once the selection lands back on the followed pass; `f` follows the running station; `⏎` opens the selection full-screen once something is actually selected. `Tab`, `z`, `v`, `s` and `l` are gone from the Flow; it keeps Cost's own key bar and panels unchanged until Cost's own part of this redesign. Below 100 columns, lanes fold and `↑↓ Lane`/`Following` drop from the key bar; under the three-pane threshold the Story pane drops too; below 80×24 the Flow isn't offered, the same floor the Signal already has.
