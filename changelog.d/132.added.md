- `cof run --profile-from-state <state.json>` reconstructs and applies the profile recorded at `runtime.effective_settings.profile`, so a past run's profile can be reproduced from that record alone — without the original `profiles/<name>.yml`, which may have changed or be gone. A record redacted for secrets refuses to reconstruct, naming the redacted field(s), rather than silently replaying the `***REDACTED***` sentinel as a real value.
